About BrushBuild

Our mission is to nurture creativity and build confidence through art education.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, BrushBuild began as a small studio with a big vision: making quality art education accessible to every child. What started as a single drawing class has grown into a comprehensive art and maker academy serving over 1,000 students annually.

Our创始人相信创造力是每个孩子与生俱来的天赋。我们的任务是提供正确的工具、指导和鼓励，帮助学生发掘他们的艺术潜能。

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do at BrushBuild.

Creativity First

We encourage unique expression and original thinking. There are no wrong answers in art, only opportunities for discovery.

Confidence Building

Every student can create. We focus on progress over perfection and celebrate every achievement along the way.

Community

We're more than a school—we're a family of artists, makers, and creative thinkers supporting each other's journeys.
